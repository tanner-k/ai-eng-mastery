Worked solutions are in `solutions/solutions.md`.

## Exercise 1 — Compute hinge loss

For margins `y f(x) = [2.0, 1.0, 0.4, -0.5]`, compute hinge losses `max(0, 1 - y f(x))`.

## Exercise 2 — Relate norm and margin

If a linear SVM has `||w|| = 4`, what is the geometric margin width between the two margin hyperplanes?

## Exercise 3 — Interpret C

Explain what happens when `C` is very large versus very small in a soft-margin SVM.

## Exercise 4 — Compare logistic regression and SVM

Both models learn linear decision boundaries. Explain one reason to prefer logistic regression and one reason to prefer a linear SVM.

## Exercise 5 — Explain the kernel trick

In your own words, explain how kernels allow nonlinear decision boundaries without explicitly constructing transformed features.
